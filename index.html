Financial summary

This is an indication only, for a sole trader on 2026 Irish rates. It is not a substitute for accountancy.

For a single sole trader, on 2026 Irish rates, using this financial year’s recorded work minus written-off invoices and the expenses you type. No other income is included. Income tax is 20% up to €44,000 and 40% after that, with a €2,000 personal credit and a €2,000 earned income credit. USC starts above €13,000, and self-employed income over €100,000 is at 11%. PRSI is Class S at 4.2%, with a €650 minimum from €5,000 of income. That rate rises to 4.35% from 1 October 2026. VAT, pensions, and other credits are left out.
