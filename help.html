How to use EverydayWork

The short path from job to paid.

  1. 1

    Log a job

    On site, open Log and record the client and what you did. Hourly uses the clock. Open Pricing to charge days × day length × the hourly rate, or to enter one job price. A job name such as Cathedral View keeps a deposit invoice and a balance invoice on the same job. Each invoice keeps its own total and pay link. Materials, hired equipment, and mileage are optional.

  2. 2

    Invoices to send

    Draft invoices sit here. Open one and read it before you send it.

  3. 3

    Mark invoiced

    When the invoice looks right, tap Mark invoiced. Email, the pay link, and the PDF stay on that invoice.

  4. 4

    Mark paid

    Open Invoices to collect, tap the invoice to check it, then tap Mark paid when the money arrives.

  5. 5

    Undo if wrong

    Finished invoices has Undo. That puts it back to collect.
