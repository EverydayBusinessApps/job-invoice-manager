How to use EverydayWork

The short path from job to paid.

  1. 1

    Log a job

    On site, open Log and record the client, what you did, and the hours.

  2. 2

    Invoices to send

    Draft invoices sit here. Open one when you are ready to bill.

  3. 3

    Email, then mark invoiced

    Email the PDF. When it has gone out, tap Mark invoiced.

  4. 4

    Mark paid

    When payment lands, open Invoices to collect and tap Mark paid.

  5. 5

    Undo if wrong

    Finished invoices has Undo. That puts it back to collect.
